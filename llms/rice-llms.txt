# Rice Business (Jesse H. Jones Graduate School of Business and Virani Undergraduate School of Business), Rice University

> Mission: Developing principled, innovative thought leaders in global communities. 

**Overview**

At Rice Business, we’re shaping the next generation of global leaders in the nation’s most diverse city. You belong here. We’re defined by our commitment to inclusivity, research and rigorous academics. Rice Business is home to both the Jones Graduate School of Business and the Virani Undergraduate School of Business at Rice University. Our culture of innovation and diversity equips students with the skills, insights and resources needed to succeed as the next generation of leaders. Located in Houston, we offer endless cultural and career opportunities. With a student body as diverse as the city of Houston and a global field experience built into the curriculum to broaden every student’s worldview, we’re the right choice to help you make your mark on the world.
